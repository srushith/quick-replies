# Privacy Policy: Quick Replies for Windows

_Last updated: 25 November 2026_

Quick Replies for Windows ("the app") is built by Srushith Kumar. This policy explains what
the app does with your information. In short: **it collects nothing and sends nothing.**

## What the app does not do

- It does **not** connect to the internet. There are no accounts, analytics, telemetry,
  crash reports, ads or update checks.
- It does **not** collect, sell or share any personal information.
- It does **not** record what you type.

## Information the app uses on your PC

To work, the app briefly uses some information locally. None of it leaves your computer.

- **Your replies and settings** are saved in a file on your PC (in your user's app-data
  folder). They are deleted when you uninstall the app.
- **The clipboard:** to insert a reply, the app puts the reply on the clipboard, presses
  Ctrl+V for you, and then puts back the text you had copied. It doesn't store or send
  clipboard contents.
- **The title of the window you're replying in** (e.g. a Slack or Discord window) is read
  at that moment to fill in `{name}` with the person's first name. It isn't stored or sent.
- **Keyboard input:** the app only simulates Ctrl+V and, if you choose, Enter. It listens
  for its own shortcut key and doesn't monitor other keystrokes.

## Children

The app doesn't collect any information from anyone, including children.
